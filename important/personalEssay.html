personal essay - draft 1



There is a context for which we experience the world; ideas, lows, morals, schemas, etc. I then take a piece of that context and place it into a box, or "frame". Animation is practicing that thousands of times-- which is why I love it. Conversation with the world --and myself-- is integral to everything I do.


Growing up in an immigrant family with brothers a generation older than me and parents inexperienced with my culture, I felt alienated. When I make robots fight or animals walk, I feel as if I'm expressing important parts of my identity, and asking important questions. What stories do I admire? What jokes do I love? What do I long for?


When I was an infant, I was starstruck by the universe. I LOVED space. Starving to learn, I started reading early and consumed book after book, wanting to grasp the stars. Today, that curiosity burns my every step. Now I am not only hungry to learn, I am hungry to be. I want to improve at everything because I want to witness the most of everything-- to renege on such desires would mean letting life pass you by. You can make anything when you animate-- I can be an astronaut, a pilot, a wizard, or a chair. Moreover, animation can never be mastered-- it is a discipline that offers limitless potential for growth alongside limitless resources for learning. While watching film or walking outside, I can observe the movement of the camera or motions of the trees. Additionally (and more specifically), there are plenty of helpful guides on Youtube or blogs, making learning easier.
