Wind Fortress

Cassiel's nadir.

Skirting the heels of the [Cassiel Division] are its watchful eyes and eager hands.

Defunct armies lay dormant, their memory flitting across the savage wind, carried in the turbulent air of the dark sky.


"The wind sometimes feels like a ghost, screaming silently across each unsettled alley... The city walls in turn cry out and jeer as they crumble, indignity pouring over and into the horizon..."
-35th century Saturnian poet



Scorch Station X

Roaring machinery charges the stale air.

Within the bowels of the [Cassiel Division] are innumerable beating hearts, designed to process crude blood into a more divine fuel, aided by the arcane power of [Mrs.C].

The flames of Scorch Station X bellow out the vengeance of those devoured.


"The discovery of clone blood refinery marked the beginning of the human Golden Age. Within months, humans established sprawling power plants and refineries on nearly all of the Saturnian moon colonies."
-51st century Extrasolar historian



The Bloodways

Red visages flood and pump souls, screeching in fury and panic.

Flowing through the veins of the [Cassiel Division] are the blood and bile of [Mrs.C]. Their powerful fumes press against the concrete walls, once pleased to carry blood through the city, now simmering with putrid air.

Some say these pipes still sound with the spirits of infidel clones. Some say none have ever made it through. Still more suggest a threat, maybe around the corner, maybe behind your back, or just out of sight...


"Clone blood, especially in its refined state, has incredibly subtle hallucinatory effects on the human mind... On a mechanical organism, however, these effects may be much more pronounced..."
- 34th century medicinal handbook



Mrs C

Malignancy permeates saturnian steel. Electric echoes of the past and present push you forward. Rise.

There were at one time rumors of a hidden god under the [Cassiel Division], drawing in life from millions of miles away.

These rumors left as the Saturnians did, but a piece of this god is still carried in the hearts of all men, and all of their children forever.


"In the 31st century, dozens of gravitational phenomena were detected on the moons of Saturn. This prompted an immediate colonization of the planet's lunar system, and the Second Great Political Dispute for interplanetary territory. This dispute resulted in an unprecedented 20 million fatalities among both Earth and Mars colonists."
-51st century Extrasolar historian



Matrioshka Space

Time slows.

Copper ichor shapes. Molds. Mutates. Exacerbates the potential of identity to the utmost degree.

Wistful life treads in reverse; the mind and heart of a soldier lost in its purpose.


"In order to keep clone labor efficient and ethical, Mrs C periodically sedates her children via their gas masks. A cloudy, labyrinthine conscious is optimal for our directive."
-33st century mechanical manual
